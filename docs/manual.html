Quick Start

  1. Flash the image / install the plugin (CLAP or VST3) into your DAW's plugin folder.
  2. Insert ChameleonConsole on a channel.
  3. Click the era label (under the VU) to cycle console eras until the tone is right.
  4. Drag the knobs: Trim, Drive, HPF/LPF, EQ, Lux/Gloss, Tape, Volume.
  5. Watch the ballistic VU; the DAW value follows the knob.

The Knobs

KnobWhat it does
TrimInput gain (±24 dB) into the strip.
DrivePreamp drive/saturation (0–100%).
HPF / LPFHigh/low-pass filter frequency.
EQ Lo/Mid/HiFrequency + gain per band.
Lux / GlossCharacter/air controls (0–1).
TapeTape emulation on/off.
VolumeOutput gain (-24…12 dB).

Era Switch & VU

The label under the VU shows the current console (e.g. Neve 1073). Click it to switch to the next era — the background, knobs and VU change to that era's set. The VU is ballistic (real attack/release) and calibrated by vu.ini.

Tuning the desk

Edit knobs.ini in an era folder to change a knob's taper/curve (linear/log/reverse) — like an old engineer tweaking the console to taste.

Install

  • CLAP: %LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\Common\CLAP\ (Win), ~/.clap/ (Linux), ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/CLAP/ (mac).
  • VST3: C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\ (Win), ~/.vst3/ (Linux), ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ (mac).

Rescan plugins in your DAW. The strip appears as Chameleon Console.
